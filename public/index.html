A CLEAR PATH TO A HELPFUL ANSWER

How the admissions guide works

Each question moves through a few careful steps. College guidance stays connected to official sources, and anything the assistant can’t confirm is sent back to the right office.

01STUDENT

Ask in your own words

Typos, follow-ups, and natural phrasing are welcome.

question
02ROUTE & RETRIEVE

Find relevant guidance

Match the question to reviewed admissions topics and official College sources.

verified topics
03ANSWER

AI or FAQ fallback

When connected, AI writes a conversational answer. Otherwise, the curated FAQ gives a source-linked response.

If a policy is unclear, point to Undergraduate Admissions.
answer + sources
04OPTIONAL · OPT IN

Save the conversation

Only after consent. The chat text is saved under a random conversation ID. The app does not ask for names, emails, or account details. Records over 30 days old are removed when a later opt-in turn is saved.

reply
05NEXT STEP

Student gets a clear next step

Official links are included. Decisions and personal records stay with the College.

Student input Assistant workflow Consent-controlled storage
Privacy by designConversation storage is optional. Don’t include Social Security numbers, CUNYfirst credentials, financial details, or private documents in chat.
