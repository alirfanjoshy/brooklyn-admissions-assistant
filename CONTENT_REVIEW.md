# Admissions template adaptation

Source: user-supplied 2020 ADMISSIONS INQUIRY TEMPLATES.docx. Original file was read only and is not included in the hosted assets.

Review date: October 6, 2026.

## Coverage

The 24 added topics cover review/checklist status, application status and decisions, late/incomplete applications, college-choice changes, declining and accepting offers, commitment deposits, summer enrollment, SMART/advisement/orientation, account activation, major declaration, SAT/ACT, English proficiency and exemptions, ESL/ALA, graduate referrals, SEEK, ASAP/ACE, nondegree and visiting study, fee waivers, tuition, credit evaluations, degree completion, and high school credential review. Existing entries cover general first-year/transfer application requirements, financial aid, international pathways, campus tours, and counselor contact.

## Changes from the historical template

- SAT/ACT: replaced mandatory score language with CUNY's policy through spring 2027. https://www.cuny.edu/admissions/undergraduate/
- ASAP: replaced the old no-program claim with Brooklyn College's current program. https://www.brooklyn.edu/cuny-asap/
- Transfer admission: retained the current requirements page's 2.30/12-credit criteria and specified 2.00 exceptions, not the 2020 2.2/24-credit language. https://www.brooklyn.edu/admissions-aid/transfer/how-to-apply/requirements-and-deadlines/
- Deposits: did not repeat the unverified $100 amount; directs to official offer and current CUNY waiver guidance. https://www.cuny.edu/admissions/new-students/
- ESL: uses current Adult Literacy information; does not present the old ALA address and phone as current. https://www.brooklyn.edu/literacy/
- Offices and appointments: current source links replace old James Hall walk-in hours and SMART screen-by-screen instructions.
- Tuition, fee waivers, nondegree and visiting pathways: current links and policies replace retired URLs.
- English test thresholds/exemptions, high-school accreditation rules, and the 15-business-day processing promise remain unconfirmed; answers explicitly refer users to Admissions.
- Staff-only records assertions, receipt acknowledgments, email forwarding promises, and inferred denials are not reproduced as chatbot actions.
- Old Core Curriculum details and two-year completion example are replaced with individual adviser/degree-audit guidance.

## Remaining limits

This is a template-based FAQ matcher. It does not understand arbitrary multi-part questions or maintain conversational context. It has no live model, student-record access, booking, payment, or message-sending integration. All topics are available directly from the searchable library when phrasing does not match. This review is not institutional approval of the content.
